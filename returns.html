Customer care

Returns & Exchanges

Effective 27 July 2026

We want every JAGAH piece to feel right. Eligible items may be returned or exchanged within 14 calendar days of receipt.

Return eligibility

To qualify, an item must be unworn, unwashed, unused and returned in its original condition with all tags and packaging intact. You must also provide valid proof of purchase.

Items showing signs of wear, washing, alteration, damage caused after receipt, perfume, cosmetics or other contamination cannot be accepted.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene and fairness, personalised items, gift cards and items marked “final sale” cannot be returned unless they arrive faulty, damaged or different from what was ordered.

Starting a return

Before sending anything back, email jagahub780@gmail.com with your order number, the item and the reason. Returns sent without prior approval may be delayed or refused.

Customers are responsible for return costs unless the item supplied was faulty, damaged or incorrect. Use a trackable service because JAGAH cannot be responsible for returns lost in transit.

Exchanges

Size or colour exchanges are subject to availability. If the requested replacement is unavailable, we will offer another eligible item or a refund. The fastest option may be to return the original item and place a new order.

Refunds

We will inspect your return and notify you of the decision. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or payment provider may require additional processing time.

Nothing in this policy limits rights you may have under applicable consumer-protection law.

Faulty or incorrect items

Inspect your order when you receive it. Report a faulty, damaged or incorrect item within 48 hours by emailing us with your order number and clear photographs. Once verified, JAGAH will arrange an appropriate replacement or refund.
