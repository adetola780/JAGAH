Privacy & Cookies

Effective 27 July 2026

This policy explains what information JAGAH collects, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when you browse, contact us, join our mailing list or place an order. This can include your name, email address, telephone number, order details, preferences and messages.

Our website may also collect device, browser, approximate location and usage information through essential cookies, logs and analytics technologies.

How we use information

  • To provide products, process orders and communicate with you.
  • To provide customer support and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To maintain, secure and improve our website and services.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.
  • To send marketing only with consent or where otherwise permitted by law.

Payments and service providers

Payment information may be handled directly by a secure payment provider. JAGAH does not need to store full card details. We may share only necessary information with trusted providers supporting payments, communications, website operations, analytics, fraud prevention and professional advice.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

Essential cookies or local storage may remember your bag, saved items and preferences. Optional analytics or advertising technologies should be used only with appropriate notice and consent where required. You can control cookies in your browser, although disabling essential storage may affect site features.

Marketing choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing at any time through the unsubscribe option in a message or by contacting us. Opting out does not stop service messages connected to an order or support request.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary, including for legal, tax, fraud-prevention and dispute-resolution needs. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction on the use of your information. You may also object to certain uses or withdraw consent. We may need to verify your identity first.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their personal information without appropriate permission.

Contact us

For a privacy question or request, email jagahub780@gmail.com. Updates to this policy will be shown by the effective date above.
