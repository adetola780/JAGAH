Customer care

Frequently Asked Questions

Updated 27 July 2026

Everything you need to know about choosing, buying and caring for your JAGAH pieces.

How do I choose the right size?

Use the measurements shown with the product and compare them with a similar garment you own. If you are between sizes or want help with the intended fit, contact us before ordering.

Are JAGAH pieces unisex?

Many JAGAH pieces have a versatile, relaxed fit. Check the individual product description and measurements because fit may vary by design.

How do I place an order?

Select your product, colour and size, add it to your bag and complete the order form. The website can prepare your order information for confirmation through WhatsApp.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Available options will be shown when your order is confirmed. Never pay through an account or link that was not confirmed through JAGAH’s official contact channel.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact us as soon as possible. We will try to help, but an order cannot be changed or cancelled after it enters processing. This does not affect applicable return rights.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Eligible unworn and unwashed items with tags may be returned or exchanged within 14 calendar days of receipt. Read our Returns & Exchanges Policy for all conditions.

What if my item is faulty or incorrect?

Email us within 48 hours of receipt with your order number and clear photographs. Once verified, we will arrange an appropriate replacement or refund.

How should I care for my JAGAH pieces?

Follow the care label. Generally, wash similar colours together on a gentle cycle, turn printed garments inside out, avoid bleach and high heat, and do not iron directly over prints.

Will sold-out pieces be restocked?

Some core items may return, while limited drops may not. Follow JAGAH on social media and join the drop list for restock and new-release announcements.

How can I contact JAGAH?

Email jagahub780@gmail.com or call 09052900067. Include your order number when asking about an existing purchase.
